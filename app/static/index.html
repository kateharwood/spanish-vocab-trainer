How it Works

The words

Words come from a list of the ~5,000 most common Spanish words, so #12 is the 12th most common. Words marked from Claude were added later.

Forms of one word (negro / negras, hablar / hablando) make a family: one card led by its main word, with the other forms hidden until you open them.

Only Levels 1–4 and Verb practice are studied.

Each session

You get one session a day, with:

  1. Every word that's due for review
  2. 10 new words, picked at random by level:

Scheduling

FSRS schedules each review for the day your chance of remembering the word drops to about 90%. Getting it right pushes the next review further out; forgetting brings it back sooner.

Progress

  • Learning: on a short repeat this session
  • Young: in review, but not yet solid for 3 weeks
  • Mature: FSRS expects you'll still remember it 3+ weeks out

Details

  • Your rating applies to every form in a family.
  • If a level runs out of new words, its share goes to the others.
  • Due and new words are shuffled together.
  • An unfinished session picks up where you left off.
  • Archived words keep their level but never come up again.
